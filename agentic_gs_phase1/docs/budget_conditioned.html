Budget-Conditioned Agentic 3DGS

A reinforcement-learning policy that takes a wall-clock training-time budget as input and adapts its whole schedule to it — how long to train, how aggressively to densify, when to refine rather than grow. One network covers any budget from seconds to full convergence, on real scenes, with a pluggable trainer (official 3DGS or the faster Faster-GS rasteriser).

The idea

Tell the agent how long it has — it plans the rest

Standard 3DGS training runs a fixed schedule to a fixed iteration count. But in practice you have a time budget, and the optimal schedule depends on it: with a few seconds you should refine the initial points and skip densification; with minutes you should grow the model and polish. We make this explicit — the budget is an input to the policy and the stopping criterion of training.

Formulation

Budget as state, terminator, and objective

1 · State

The 45-dim block state gains a budget channel — the policy reads its absolute budget and what fraction remains:

$$s_k \;\ni\; \Big(\tfrac{B}{B_{\max}},\; \tfrac{t_k}{B},\; 1-\tfrac{t_k}{B}\Big)$$

\(t_k\) = elapsed training seconds after block \(k\).

2 · Termination

The wall-clock budget is the stopping criterion; the episode ends the first block after the budget is spent (or a hard 30k-iteration cap for the unlimited case):

$$\text{done}_k \;=\; \big[\, t_k \ge B \,\big]$$

The stop head no longer terminates — the clock does.

3 · Reward

Dense per-block quality gain (which telescopes to final quality) plus a terminal bonus for the quality reached at the budget; no time penalty:

$$r_k = \Delta q_k \; d_k \;+\; \mathbb{1}[\text{done}_k]\,\beta\,(q_k-q_0)$$

\(\Delta q_k\) block quality gain, \(d_k\) compactness discount, \(\beta{=}0.5\).

This reshaping is what makes budget-conditioning work: an earlier design that only rewarded final quality at the 30k cap (never reached under a time budget) and charged a per-block time penalty produced agents that stopped almost immediately and ignored the budget. Removing the time penalty and paying the quality bonus at budget exhaustion made the dense quality signal drive the agent to spend its budget well.

What it learns

Densify only when there is time to

The clearest sign of genuine budget-awareness is how the agent spends a tight budget. With only 30 s the 3DGS agent chooses not to densify at all — it refines the initial point cloud as fast as possible — then densifies progressively more as the budget grows. The number of blocks in which densification is active climbs directly with the budget:

  • Tight budget → skip growth, refine the seed geometry, stop when the clock runs out.
  • Generous budget → grow the model, then polish appearance — the classic shape-then-refine curriculum, timed to the budget.
  • One policy, no retraining, for any budget in between.
Budget-conditioned Agentic 3DGS — held-out real-scene evaluation. Agents trained on 8 COLMAP scenes (T&T / Deep Blending / Mip-NeRF 360), 150 PPO updates, budget \(B\sim\mathcal{U}[20,300]\)s; evaluated at 30/60/120/300/360 s and unlimited (30k).
