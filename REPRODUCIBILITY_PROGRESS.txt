8. NEW BACKEND: DashGaussian (Chen et al., CVPR 2025) (2026-07-31)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  User: implement DashGaussian as a 3rd trainer_backend to test policy optimization.
  DECISIONS: controllable substrate (policy drives densify/prune/LR on top of Dash's
    schedule); PRAGMATIC (Dash scheduling on existing 3dgs rasterizer, no CUDA build);
    BACKEND + SMOKE TEST FIRST (stop for review before any full train/eval).
  WHAT DASH IS: 3DGS + (1) resolution schedule (render training views coarse->fine,
    freq-energy paced) + (2) momentum primitive budget (caps densification, suppressed
    at low res). License CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 -> we REIMPLEMENT (not vendor) with attribution.
  IMPLEMENTATION:
    - agentic_gs_phase1/envs/backends/dash_schedule.py: DashScheduler (res_scale +
      primitive_budget). Resolution = coarse->fine ramp, exponent gamma from FFT
      frequency concentration, reaches full res at res_full_fraction*max_steps (0.9).
      Budget = momentum m<-max(m, 0.98m + 0.2*count*scale^2), caps growth early/low-res.
    - trainer_backends.py: DashGaussianBackend(ThreeDGSBackend), name="dash", is_dash=True,
      wraps gaussian-splatting (reuses 3dgs render). Registered in _REGISTRY.
    - gs_env.py (all dash-gated, no-op for 3dgs/fastergs): dash_enabled flag; build
      DashScheduler at reset from train images; _dash_scaled_inputs() renders + computes
      loss at scheduled resolution (shallow-copy camera w/ scaled dims + bilinear GT);
      densify budget cap; resolution obs channel now dynamic (= _current_res_scale).
